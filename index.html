About Us

Crafted for Bidholi's student rhythm and hillside spirit.

The Daily Grind wasn't born from a corporate boardroom; it emerged from the student energy of Bidholi. We recognized that university life demands more than just generic caffeine; it requires a welcoming space, affordable fuel, and a taste of home, right here in the Dehradun hills. We are a community hub designed for focused study sessions, quick breaks between classes, and casual hangouts with friends, offering a relaxed hillside vibe that fits the rhythm of student life.

Our menu reflects this dual identity. We serve expertly crafted coffee to keep you sharp, alongside a unique selection of authentic Uttarakhand Pahadi comfort food. From hearty Gahat Dal Soup and Mandua Roti with Sisunak Saag to innovative twists like our Kandalee Ka Saag Sandwich and Bal Mithai Cheesecake, we bring the rich flavors of the Himalayas to your table. Whether you're fueling up for an exam or unwinding after a long day, The Daily Grind is your go-to spot for great taste, affordable prices, and genuine local hospitality.
